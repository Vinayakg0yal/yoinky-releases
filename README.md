# Yoinky

A fast, modern download manager: multi-connection downloads, videos from YouTube and
1,000+ sites in every quality (up to 4K and MP3), full-size images, an AI upscaler,
background removal and a download queue.

## Download

Get the latest version from **[Releases](https://github.com/Vinayakg0yal/yoinky-releases/releases/latest)**.

- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `Yoinky_x.y.z_aarch64.dmg`. Open it and drag Yoinky into Applications.
- **Windows:** coming soon.

The first time you open it on a Mac, right-click Yoinky in Applications and choose **Open**,
then **Open** again (Yoinky isn't notarized by Apple yet).

## Updates

Yoinky checks for new versions by itself and shows **Update now** when one is out.
Updating installs over your current version and keeps your downloads and settings.
You can also check from Settings → Updates.
